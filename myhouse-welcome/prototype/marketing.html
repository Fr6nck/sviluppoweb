Questions

Before you buy.

Do my guests need to install anything?

No. Your guide is a web page. They tap the link or scan the QR code and it opens — on any phone, any tablet, any computer. There is nothing to download and no account to create.

What happens to my printed QR code if I change something?

Nothing. The QR code points to a permanent address that we resolve to your current guide. You can rewrite every word, change your guide's web address, even move to your own domain later — the code you printed and stuck on the fridge keeps working.

I'm not good with computers. Is this going to be hard?

You answer questions about your home, one screen at a time. "What time can guests check in?" "Is there Wi-Fi?" You can skip anything, save and come back later, and do the whole thing from your phone. Most hosts finish in about twenty minutes.

Can I do it in more than one language?

Yes. Essential covers one language. Plus lets you write in up to five yourself. Pro translates everything automatically and lets you correct it before your guests see it — and a correction you make is never overwritten by a later translation.

Where do I put the Wi-Fi password and the door code?

In the guide, where guests can copy them with one tap. Wi-Fi gets its own screen with a copy button and a QR code that joins the network. With Pro you can also hide the door code behind a short PIN that you give the guest.

Can I change my guide after I publish it?

Whenever you like, as often as you like. Changes are saved as you type and go live when you press Publish. Guests always see the published version, so you can edit at three in the morning without anyone seeing a half-finished sentence.

Do you track my guests?

No. We set no cookies on your guide and store nothing about the people who read it. You can see how many times your guide was opened and which sections were used — that's about the guide, not about your guests.

I have more than one property.

Pro covers up to five, and you can copy a finished guide to a new property and change the details rather than starting again. If you manage more than five, email us and we'll sort something out.

What if I want a refund?

Email us within 14 days and we'll refund you. Your guide goes offline but nothing you wrote is deleted — if you come back, it's all still there.
